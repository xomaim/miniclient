Mini Client Terms of Service

1. What Mini Client is. Mini Client is a free, unofficial launcher and client mod for Minecraft: Java Edition. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft.

2. Your Minecraft account. You sign in with your own Microsoft account through Microsoft's official sign-in page. Mini Client never sees your password. Your login is stored only on this PC, encrypted by Windows, and is used only to start Minecraft. You can sign out at any time in Settings.

3. You must own Minecraft. Mini Client does not give you the game. You need a Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition to play online.

4. Fair play. Mini Client is for performance, menus and cosmetics. Do not use it, or mods you add to it, to cheat or break the rules of any server. Servers may ban you for their own reasons, and Mini Client is not responsible for that.

5. Downloads. Mini Client downloads Minecraft files from Mojang, Fabric from FabricMC, mods from Modrinth and its own files from GitHub. Third-party mods keep their own licences.

6. Updates. Mini Client updates itself automatically to fix problems and add features.

7. No warranty. Mini Client is provided "as is", without any warranty. Use it at your own risk. Keep backups of worlds you care about.

8. Privacy. Mini Client does not collect or sell personal data. It contacts only the services above to sign you in, download files and show server icons and skins.

9. Changes. These terms may change. If they do, you will be asked to accept them again.
